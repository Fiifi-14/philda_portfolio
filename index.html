About me

I'm a Registered Community Health Nurse based in Akim Achiase, Eastern Region, Ghana, currently pursuing a BSc in Midwifery at Garden City University (2026 – 2027). I trained at the Pantang Nursing Training and Midwifery school, graduating in August 2017 with a focus on Anatomy and Physiology, Midwifery, and Pediatrics.

My academic journey began at Akim Swedru Senior High School, where I completed General Arts studies in Christian Religious Studies, Literature, French, and Government, graduating in June 2011.

Over the past eight years, I have built hands-on clinical experience across preventive care, hospital wards, and community health — serving as a Community Health Nurse at Suhum Government Hospital since September 2020, alongside earlier clinical internships at Nsawam Government Hospital and St. Dominic's – Ghana Government Hospital. I have also contributed to national civic duty as a Verification Officer with the Electoral Commission of Ghana.
